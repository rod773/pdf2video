Just launched: PDF to Video Generator

Transform any PDF into a dynamic video presentation in seconds.

What it does:
- Upload a PDF and get a video with animated slides, transitions, and text effects
- Fully client-side — no server processing, no data leaves your browser
- Download as MP4 or WebM

Built with vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. Containerized with Docker. Deployed on Vercel.

Live demo: https://pdf2video-chi.vercel.app
Source code: https://github.com/rod773/pdf2video
